## Brand & Style

AeroGrid serves infrastructure investors, urban planners, regional aviation authorities, and fleet operators planning vertiport and eVTOL corridors across the Indian subcontinent. The aesthetic translates the physical elegance and systematic precision of a pilot’s sectional chart into a calm, authoritative digital cockpit.

The visual style is **Tactile Precision / Modern Cartographic**:
- **Tactile Paper Surfaces:** Warm cream foundations evoke durable aviation chart substrates, paired with stark white structural modules to avoid sterile software genericism.
- **Instrumental Restraint:** Every border, tick mark, and rule serves an informational purpose, echoing coordinates, altimeter tapes, and visual flight rules (VFR) navigation plates.
- **Deliberate Micro-Interactions:** Subtle haptic-feeling feedback, crisp borders, and monospaced telemetry ensure rapid readability under high-density analytical conditions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged operational reading, referencing high-grade cartographic inks and regulatory aviation markings.

### Core Palette
- **Canvas / Substrate (`#F6F3EA`):** Warm paper cream background serving as the foundational surface.
- **Card / Surface Container (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density optical white for analytical tiles, data matrices, and operational cards.
- **Chart Blue (`#2F6FBA`):** Primary accent. Used for verified corridors, active navigation paths, vertical lift nodes, and primary system states.
- **Deep Ink Navy (`#14213D`):** Primary typography and prominent chart vectors. Replaces harsh synthetic black for an ink-on-vellum finish.
- **Muted Slate (`#5B6475`):** Secondary typography, metadata descriptors, units of measure, and inactive vector geometry.
- **Aviation Orange (`#E8590C`):** High-visibility highlight. Reserved for operational constraints, terrain warnings, airspace conflicts, and primary user intervention triggers.
- **Paper Border Line (`#DDD6C8`):** Delicate warm-grey line weight for layout separators, grid modules, and micro-delimiters.

### Operational Verdicts & Telemetry
- **Viable (`#1F9D6B`):** Cleared for vertiport siting or corridor licensing.
- **Conditional (`#D98E04`):** Airspace review, micro-weather hazards, or grid capacity restrictions.
- **Not Viable (`#C8372D`):** Obstacle collision slope infringement, prohibited airspace, or prohibitive gradient.

## Typography

Typography establishes an unambiguous informational hierarchy via three coordinated font roles:

1. **Space Grotesk (Architectural & Section Titles):** Used for platform modules, site evaluation names, corridor identities, and key modal headers. Its geometric structure provides an engineered, mid-century aeronautical demeanor.
2. **Inter (Interface & Analytical Narrative):** Governs operational body copy, assessment summaries, regulatory clauses, and continuous documentation. Tuned for dense layout legibility.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Telecommunication):** Strictly applied to ICAO/IATA station tags (`VIDP`, `VABB`, `BLR`), geographic coordinates (`28°34'07"N 77°06'44"E`), runway headings, elevation figures, approach slope angles, and rating matrices. Always rendered in tabular numerals to maintain columnar integrity across moving dynamic readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

AeroGrid employs a calibrated 12-column fluid-hybrid grid with cartographic margin rules.

- **Breakpoints & Grids:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`gutter-lg`) gutters, 40px (`margin-lg`) canvas margins. Side inspectors and map canvas operate in a docked multi-pane split.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`gutter`) gutters, 24px (`margin-md`) margins. Auxiliary analytical panels collapse into overlay trays.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters, 16px (`margin`) margins. Map-centric bottom sheet paradigm for vertiport site inspections.

- **Rhythm & Cartographic Separators:**
  Components align to an internal 4px structural baseline. Structural regions are separated using fine 1px line rules (`#DDD6C8`) accented with corner ticks (`+` markers at grid intersections) to reinforce the drafted blueprint aesthetic without adding visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile surface layering and delicate boundary contrast rather than heavy diffusion:

- **Substrate Floor:** The `#F6F3EA` warm paper cream base serves as Elevation 0.
- **Structural Modules (Elevation 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` cards outlined with a 1px solid `#DDD6C8` border. Finished with an ultra-subtle ambient shadow tinted with warm navy: `0 1px 3px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Heads-Up Displays (Elevation 2):** Flight corridor tooltips, waypoint selectors, and map inspector drawers utilize crisp `#FFFFFF` with a reinforced border (`1px solid #C8BFB0`) and focused ambient elevation: `0 4px 16px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Modals & Critical Intervention Sheets (Elevation 3):** Grounded by a warm translucent ink scrim (`rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.35)` with a 2px micro-blur) to sustain situational map context while focusing input.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a controlled 10px baseline geometry:

- **Cards & Data Modules:** 10px corner radius (`rounded`) produces a crisp, finished technical instrument feel.
- **Buttons & Tactical Inputs:** 10px corner radius ensures ergonomic cohesion with panel containers.
- **Tags & Status Badges:** 6px radius (`rounded-sm`) for compact geometric nesting inside tables and card headers.
- **Cartographic Schemas (Vertiport Markers):**
  - **Paved Facilities / Hardened Vertipads:** Rounded squares (10px corner geometry) in accent blue or verdict tones.
  - **Unpaved / Natural / Emergency Clearances:** Diamond configurations (45° rotated squares with 2px corner fillets).
